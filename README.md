## The word

**Corridance** — the awkward sidestep dance when two people keep blocking each other in a hallway.

Everyone knows the moment: you meet someone coming the other way in a corridor, you both step left, then both step right, then left again — a brief, silent, mutually choreographed dance with no name. *Corridance* (corridor + dance) gives it one: sayable, spellable, and instantly recognisable.
